# Version 0.3 — Dual-view reference-informed beta

- Preserves the visual design and the full musician controls from 0.2.
- Adds **Simple View** (default) with one-click microphone Start/Stop, musical feel, optional saved song, and example groove preview.
- Adds **Musician View** to reveal all existing advanced controls.
- Adds **Highlife praise** and **Fast Ariaria praise** original accompaniment programs alongside Worship and Praise.
- Approximate pulse candidates in 15-second excerpts informed tempo suggestions: highlife ~129 BPM, Ariaria ~152 BPM; choose by ear, not as exact transcriptions.
- No source audio is included, scraped, streamed, uploaded, or used for model training. All styles are original rule-based patterns, not performer recreations.
- Automatic key/chord following is STILL experimental. Not suitable for unattended live services.
