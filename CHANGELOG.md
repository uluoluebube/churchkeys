# ChurchKeys v0.4 — Service Flow & Bass Lab

- Added dedicated plucked electric bass synthesis with adjustable bass volume and quick ON/OFF. Separate from piano tone.
- Added short bass approaches toward the next known chord in Guided / demonstration mode; fifths and octave movement in praise modes.
- Added a soft pad underlay for gentle worship chords.
- Added **Auto follow (experimental)** style option. It proposes worship, praise, highlife, or Ariaria based on sustained estimates of the live beat tempo, with style changes only on bar boundaries. Does not recognize songs/sections or guarantee musical appropriateness.
- Added **Hear service transitions** demonstration (cycles through styles every 2 bars). This is a programmed demonstration, not evidence of auto audio recognition.
- Introduced tempo estimate smoothing to reduce transient shifts.
- Retains Simple and Musician Views, UI layout, microphone access and optional saved arrangements.
- The 21 references were previously measured for approximate pulse/rhythmic categories only. No transcription, model training, stem separation, or embedding of copyrighted recordings.
